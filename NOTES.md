# Design Notes

The v21 layout treats the cream page as a tactile newsroom artifact: every story card has a flat-vector art band, and rubber stamps mark the page with an urgent, selected, filed feel. A dedicated “WHERE IT HAPPENED” map panel uses numbered pins that mirror the 33 story cards, letting readers move from each place to its matching report without decoding a second legend. The bold Thursday 19 November 2026 masthead, strong rules, restrained ink colours, and date-check footer keep the dense global edition readable while separating that day’s events from context carried over from another news cycle.

The five-phase layout moves from edition setup to map orientation, lead stories, regional briefs, and a final date-check close.
