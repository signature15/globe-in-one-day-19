# Design Notes

The numbered map pins mirror the story numbers so readers can move from a place on the globe to its matching report without decoding a second legend. Around that geographic index, the cream broadsheet balances a bold Thursday 19 November 2026 masthead with 33 stories arranged from lead coverage to shorter reports; strong rules and restrained ink colours keep the groups distinct, while the date-check footer separates this Thursday’s events from context carried over from another news cycle.

The five-phase layout moves from edition setup to map orientation, lead stories, regional briefs, and a final date-check close.
